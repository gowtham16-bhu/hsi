# Decision 0003: from precomputed full chains to shortcut hops (RQ1′)

**Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Status:** accepted · **Affects:** 00-thesis (revision 1), C5, C7, 04 Gate 0′

## What failed, and where it points
Gate 0a (gate0.md) failed. Each failure point becomes an improvement target:

| Failure point (evidence) | Improvement target |
|---|---|
| 69% of MuSiQue 3-hop gold chains never generated by a question-independent beam; the number of possible chains grows as m^L | Do not precompute 3–4 hop chains. Precompute 2-passage shortcuts only (2-hop pruning was 15%) and do the remaining hops at query time |
| When a gold chain *is* stored, one lookup finds it ~87% of the time | Keep the unified shortcut index; it is the part that works |
| 16–29% of gold chains never linked in the graph | Supporting work on C3/C4 (entity linking, aliases); measured separately by the waterfall |
| HSI latency 39 ms vs 5 ms (exact flat search over 335K entries) | ANN index plugin; not a thesis claim |
| Only one weak baseline (expand-vec); external LLM-free systems report large gains from query-time graph steps | Add strong LLM-free baselines before any claim: PPR from dense seeds, and iterative passage hops |

Research basis: TreeHop (arXiv 2504.20114) and MDR do later hops at query time without an LLM;
GRIEVER (EMNLP 2025 Industry) does LLM-free multi-step graph retrieval; IndexRAG (2603.16415)
precomputes pairwise bridges, not full chains.

## RQ1′ (pre-registered before any run)
> With 2-passage shortcuts in the index, an iterative LLM-free retriever needs about half the lookups:
> hopping over shortcuts with 2 lookup rounds retrieves deep (3–4 hop) evidence better than hopping
> over passages with 2 rounds, and as well as hopping over passages with 4 rounds.

Systems (all LLM-free, same encoder, same graph, same passage budget):
- `hop-passages-2`, `hop-passages-4`: iterative retrieval over the passage index, 2 or 4 lookup rounds
- `hop-shortcuts-2`: the same algorithm over the passage + 2-passage-shortcut index, 2 rounds
- `ppr`: personalized PageRank over the graph from dense seeds
- `expand-vec`, `dense`, `bm25`: as in Gate 0a

**Pass rule (Gate 0′), on fresh questions (no overlap with the Gate 0a sample), MuSiQue, slice = 3- and 4-hop questions together, full-chain R@10:**
1. `hop-shortcuts-2` − `hop-passages-2` ≥ 5 points, 95% CI low > 0, **and**
2. `hop-shortcuts-2` − `hop-passages-4` ≥ −2 points at the CI low end (non-inferior), **and**
3. `hop-shortcuts-2` ≥ the best of `ppr` and `expand-vec` (CI low ≥ 0).

2Wiki is reported with the same rule on its 4-hop slice. A fail sends the work back to this table.

## Fixed settings (pre-registered, written before the first Gate 0′ run)
- Encoder, graph and extractor exactly as Gate 0a (`configs/gate0/*-0a.yaml`). Shortcuts: 2-passage
  chains only (`max_passages: 2`), all candidates stored, mean-of-vectors keys.
- Hop systems: `update: vec`, `vec_weight: 1.0`, `beam: 5`, `per_round: 10`. The text update
  (MDR-style re-encoding) is a secondary run on a subsample, because it costs ~15 encoder passes per
  question on this machine.
- PPR: `seeds: 5`, `restart: 0.5`, `iterations: 50`, `tolerance: 1e-6`.
- expand-vec: `seeds: 5`, `beam: 3`, `max_passages: 4`.
- Fresh questions: 1,000 sampled with seed 1 after excluding the 1,000 Gate 0a questions (seed 0).
- Passage budget k = 10; 1,000 bootstrap resamples; paired differences on the same questions.
